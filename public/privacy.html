In short

In short

  • This site sets no cookies.
  • We count visits anonymously, without profiles and without following you across sites.
  • All scripts and styles are served from this site; no advertising or social media trackers are loaded.
  • Automated scraping and the use of this site's content to train AI systems are not permitted.
Controller

Who we are

Undead Networks (horrordigitalhumanities.org) is a research project run by Anca-Simina Martin and Andrei-Nicolae Martin-Căluțiu in Sibiu, Romania. For anything related to this page, write to andreinicolae@tuta.io.

Cookies

Cookies

We do not set cookies, and we do not load third-party content that would set them. The notice you see on your first visit is there to inform you, not to ask for consent: there is nothing to consent to.

Statistics

Visitor statistics

To know how many people use the archive and which analyses they open, we use Vercel Web Analytics. It works without cookies. For each page view it records the page, the referring site, the approximate country, and the type of browser, operating system and device. Visitors are told apart by a hash computed from the request, which changes every day, so a visitor cannot be recognised from one day to the next or across websites. We only see aggregate figures.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding, in aggregate, how the project's site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Hosting

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse and automated scraping, Vercel's servers necessarily process technical data such as your IP address and browser identifier. We do not use these data to identify visitors. Vercel processes them as our provider, under its data processing terms; its servers may be located outside the European Union.

Your browser

What we keep in your browser

Only one thing: once you close the privacy notice, your browser's local storage remembers it (the date you closed it), so the notice does not appear again. It never leaves your device, and you can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Email

If you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and, if you join the project, to work with you. We keep the correspondence for as long as the collaboration or the conversation requires.

Your rights

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of personal data we hold about you, object to its processing, or ask us to restrict it. Write to andreinicolae@tuta.io. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

Terms of use

Using our content

The analyses, visualisations, datasets and texts on this site are the work of the Undead Networks team.

You are welcome to

  • read and explore the analyses;
  • use them in teaching and research, and quote or show them with a citation (see How to cite);
  • export individual views (SVG or PNG) for presentations and publications, with a citation.

Not permitted without our written agreement

  • scraping, crawling or bulk downloading of the site or its data, by any automated means;
  • using the site's content or data to train, fine-tune, evaluate or feed artificial intelligence systems, or to build datasets;
  • republishing the datasets behind the visualisations.

Reservation of text and data mining rights

We expressly reserve the rights of text and data mining over this site and its content, under Art. 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 on copyright in the Digital Single Market and the national laws implementing it. The reservation is also expressed in machine-readable form: in robots.txt, in the tdm-reservation and X-Robots-Tag headers sent with every page, and in our TDMRep policy.

Researchers who need the data for a study are welcome to ask us at andreinicolae@tuta.io.
